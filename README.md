# <font color="cyan" size="7">Task Sheet 9.1.1 - Build a Food Brand with AI (2D and 3D logo)</font>

## Overview

This task sheet accompanies **Lecture 9: AI Image Processing, Generation & Prompt Engineering**. Students will create an original food or beverage brand, write a clear product description and brand concept, and use **advanced prompts** to generate both a **2D logo** and a **3D logo** with AI image generation tools.

## Learning Objectives

* Design an original food brand identity, product description, and visual concept.
* Apply structured, advanced prompt engineering principles for clean **2D** brand marks.
* Adapt prompt details (depth, materials, lighting, and render cues) to generate **3D** brand logos.
* Use negative prompts and iterative adjustments to improve output quality.

## What Is Included in This Activity

1. **Brand Name & Product Description** — A short, clear, and appetizing summary of the food or drink product.
2. **Brand Concept & Target Audience** — The brand vibe, target customer, and 2–4 core brand colors.
3. **2D Logo Advanced Prompt & Output** — Positive prompt + generated 2D logo image.
4. **3D Logo Advanced Prompt & Output** — Positive prompt + generated 3D logo image.
5. **Short Reflection** — A brief comparison of how the prompt changed from 2D to 3D.

## How to Complete the Task Sheet

1. **Create Your Brand & Product:** Choose a food or drink item, write a 2–3 sentence product description, and define your concept and color palette.
2. **Craft the 2D Logo Prompt:** Focus on clean shapes, flat 2D styling, solid colors, centered framing, and negative terms that block 3D effects or mockups.
3. **Craft the 3D Logo Prompt:** Describe depth, bevels, surface materials, studio lighting, and negative terms that block flat 2D or low-quality outputs.
4. **Generate & Refine:** Test your prompts, make targeted adjustments one detail at a time, and insert your prompts, images, and reflection into the worksheet.
